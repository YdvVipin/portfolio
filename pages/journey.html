My Journey

From RF drive-tests in Gurgaon to architecting AI agent platforms for a Toronto team — a look at how one career kept turning left toward automation and AI.

6 Milestones · 8+ Years · 1 Remote-First Career

The Milestones

Every pivot built on the last — from field engineering, to QA, to AI.

📡
May 2015 — Feb 2017
📍 Gurgaon, India
The Signal
Test Engineer, RF Network Engineering — Mobilecomm Technologies

It started with radio waves, not test scripts. Chasing dropped calls across GSM, UMTS, and VoLTE networks, I spent my days buried in drive-test logs, hunting for the exact spot a signal died. When Airtel rolled out 4G in 2015, I was in the field for it — and somewhere between the spreadsheets and the scripts I wrote to speed up log analysis, I discovered I liked automating the search more than doing it by hand. That instinct would define the next decade.

GSM/UMTS/VoLTE RF Analysis Scripting
📱
Feb 2017 — Nov 2019
📍 Gurgaon, India
Into QA
QA Test Engineer — OPPO Mobiles

At OPPO, testing stopped being a side skill and became the job. I learned Appium and Selenium the way you learn a new language — by breaking things in Java until they worked. The scripting habit from the RF days paid off fast: testing strategies I built helped cut customer complaints by 75% across India in a single year, and I ended up training over 100 engineers on the process. This was the moment QA automation stopped being a task and became a craft.

Appium Selenium Java Mobile QA
🤖
Jul 2020 — Dec 2020
📍 Delhi, India
First AI Build
QA Analyst — Sofbang LLC

Sofbang handed me a blank slate: build an automation framework from zero for an AI chatbot serving a major North American power utility. No legacy code to lean on, no existing pipeline — just Selenium, Java, Cucumber, and Jenkins, and a deadline. It was the first time I was testing something genuinely "intelligent," and it planted a question I couldn't shake: what does quality even mean when the thing under test is making its own decisions?

Selenium Cucumber Jenkins AI Chatbot
🎓
Jan 2021 — Dec 2022
📍 Toronto, Canada
The Leap
Post-Graduate Diploma, Computer Programming — Seneca College

That question was worth crossing an ocean for. I left a comfortable career in India and moved to Toronto to go back to school — betting two years of tuition and uncertainty on the idea that QA and AI were about to become inseparable. Seneca gave me the formal computer science foundation the field engineer in me never had: data structures, systems design, modern programming — the missing half of the toolkit.

Computer Programming Toronto, Canada
🚀
Jan 2023 — Present
📍 Toronto, Ontario
Leading at Scale
QA Automation Specialist — Bright Order Inc.

The bet paid off. At Bright Order, I've gone from writing test cases to architecting the systems that write and run them — an 18-agent orchestration platform, 72 FastAPI endpoints, a Playwright BDD framework with a 9-tier locator system smart enough to find UI elements humans would miss, and a RAG knowledge layer that answers QA questions with 95%+ accuracy. This is where the RF engineer, the manual tester, and the AI-curious grad student finally became one job title.

18-Agent Orchestration Playwright BDD RAG FastAPI
AI-Enabled QA Platform → Playwright BDD Framework → QA RAG System →
🌍
Currently
2023 — Today
📍 Remote — India, serving Toronto & beyond
Now: Building Without Borders
Independent AI Builder & QA Automation Specialist — Bright Order Inc. (remote) + independent projects

These days I write code from India for a team headquartered in Toronto — and the work hasn't missed a beat. Async standups, overlapping hours, clean handoffs: distance turned out to be a solved problem, not a limitation. It's proof I can deliver for a North American company from anywhere in the world. In the hours outside my day job, I'm building independently — 11 AI-powered QA projects and counting, from multi-agent orchestration to RAG-based knowledge systems — because the tools I wished existed as a junior tester, I can finally build myself. Next chapter: a remote-first role, full-time or contract, with a team in Canada, the US, or Europe.
